# Ley Line Finder

Find ley lines between sacred and ancient places — stone circles, burial mounds, old churches, runestones, sacred springs, mountains and more — on a flat map or a 3D globe.

**Open the app:** https://deviagentrix.github.io/leyline-finder/

## What it does

- **Ley lines** — finds straight alignments of sacred sites and ranks them by how much stronger they are than chance.
- **Sites** — browse and search every saved site by name, type, country and distance from you.
- **Discover** — pick any two sites and see what else lies on the line between them, even all the way around the Earth.
- **Famous lines** — St Michael's Line and the Apollo–St Michael line.
- **Google Maps and Google Earth** buttons on every site and line, plus a file you can open in Google Earth.
- **Works offline** — sites are saved on your device, and map areas can be saved for trips.
- **Location** is only used while the app is open, and never leaves your device.

## Install on a phone

- **iPhone / iPad:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then *Install app*.
